Online play survives a dropped connection. The terminal reconnects at once and then with backoff, your match keeps playing meanwhile, and back within a few seconds it carries on as if nothing happened; back later, you watch the rest of the match. A seat in a room's lobby is held for 30 s. Joining a full room, or one mid-match, makes you a spectator who sees every board as it stands, and takes a free seat when the match ends.
