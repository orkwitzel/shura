Play online from the terminal's own screens. *Play online* in the main menu (and `shura quick`, `shura create [--mode ffa]` and `shura join <CODE>`) asks for a nickname the first time and saves it, then opens a menu of Quick match, Create room (1v1 or FFA) and Join room, saying when it can't reach the server, with Retry. Quick match waits with a timer, and Esc cancels. The lobby shows the room's code and link, every seat with its `guest` or `bot` tag, the host, who is ready and their last place, the spectators, your ping and who came and went; R or Enter readies (Enter starts, for the host), S spectates or takes a seat, N changes your nickname. In a match, each well has a `▐` garbage meter, the HUD shows lines sent, pieces per second, the players still in and your ping (or "Reconnecting…"), attacks flash their target's name, out boards grey with their place, and Esc or P opens a menu that doesn't pause, whose *Leave match* leaves the room. Spectators see every board, and the results list every place with lines sent, attack per minute and pieces per second (Rematch or Leave after a quick match). The settings screen has a nickname row. Server errors read in plain words.
