Online play survives a dropped connection: the client reconnects at once, then every 250 ms for 3 s, then every 2 s, as the same session, back in its seat with its nickname. A match plays on meanwhile, and back within 3 s it carries on as if nothing happened; later, you watch the rest. Joining a room mid-match shows every board as it is.
