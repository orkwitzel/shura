"shura": minor
---

Play Sprint, Marathon and Ultra offline in the terminal. `shura` opens a main menu (Single player with each mode's personal best, Settings, Quit), and `shura solo sprint|marathon|ultra` starts a game straight away. Both the Kitty keyboard protocol and plain terminals work, with the input adapter chosen from the first key (or fixed with `--input precise|basic`). Settings (handling, key bindings, ghost, theme and skin) and personal bests are kept between runs. `quick`, `create` and `join` parse but say online play isn't available yet.
