Online matches now keep to the server's clock: a player more than 3 s behind is lagged out, and one whose game runs ahead of the server is removed. Your game stops as soon as the server says you're out, and you watch the rest. Placements follow the frame each player went out at, so a finish isn't decided by whose batches arrived first, and two players out at the same frame share a place.
